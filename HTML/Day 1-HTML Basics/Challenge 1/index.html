Is the Growth of AI Hunting Youngsters' Jobs?

Published: July 2026 | Tech & Society Section

The Rise of Automation

Artificial Intelligence has advanced at an unprecedented pace. What started as basic automation has now transformed into systems capable of writing code, designing graphics, and generating complex reports.

For young professionals entering the workforce, this shift raises a critical question: Are entry-level jobs disappearing?

What the Experts Think

Many industry analysts argue that AI isn't killing jobs, but rather changing them. However, a recent study shows that 45% of fresh graduates feel anxious about their job security. You can read more about this study on the official Tech Outlook Report.
